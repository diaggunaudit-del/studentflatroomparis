FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a French guarantor? +
No. We do not require a French guarantor (garant français). International students, Erasmus students, and foreign young professionals are all welcome to apply directly.
Are bills included in the rent? +
Yes. Electricity, water, heating, and high-speed WiFi are all included in the monthly rent. You pay one fixed price each month — no surprises.
What is the minimum rental period? +
We offer flexible contracts from 1 month to 12 months, making us ideal for short Erasmus stays, internships, or full academic years.
How far are the rooms from central Paris? +
Our Gagny residence (RER E — Gagny station) is 25–35 minutes from Gare du Nord. Our Rosny-sous-Bois residence (RER A — Rosny-sous-Bois station) is 20–30 minutes from Paris city centre.
Can I book online before arriving in France? +
Absolutely. You can complete the entire booking process online: fill the application form, pay the deposit securely via PayPal, and sign your contract digitally. No need to be in France.
What documents do I need to provide? +
We typically ask for a copy of your student card or acceptance letter, a valid ID or passport, and proof of income or scholarship. Contact us on WhatsApp and we'll guide you through the process.
